
# BillBook Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

BillBook is an offline bill and dues tracker for Android and iPhone, published by **Aarutech** ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## The short version

- BillBook has no account, no sign-in and no server. Your bills, payments, people and photos are stored only on your phone.
- We do not collect, sell or share your bills, photos or personal information.
- Bill scanning runs on your phone using Google ML Kit. Google ML Kit sends limited technical diagnostics to Google (described below), but not your photos or the text on your bills.

## Information stored on your phone

Everything you enter or scan stays in the app's private storage on your device:

- Bills and payments: names of payees, suppliers or customers you type, amounts, dates, categories, notes and line items.
- Bill photos you take or pick, and the text read from them.
- Settings: your currency label and whether you last used Personal or Business mode.

We cannot see or access this information. It is deleted when you delete a bill in the app, or all of it when you uninstall BillBook or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

## Camera and photos

BillBook asks for camera access only when you choose "Scan with camera", and uses the system photo picker when you choose "Pick photo from gallery". Photos are used to read the bill on your phone and are kept with that bill. You can use BillBook without allowing camera access by entering bills by hand.

## Google ML Kit (bill scanning)

Text recognition is done on your device by Google ML Kit. According to Google's [ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure), ML Kit sends Google the following for diagnostics and usage analytics, encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and Google does not transfer it to third parties:

- Device information (manufacturer, model, operating system version, available machine-learning hardware).
- App information (package name and app version).
- A per-installation identifier that is not intended to identify you or your device.
- Performance and error information (for example how long recognition took, image size and format, error codes).

This does not include your photos, the text on your bills or anything you type. Google's use of this data is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Exporting and sharing

When you use "Export CSV", BillBook creates a file on your phone and opens your phone's share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send it (for example email, Google Drive or a messaging app), and that service's own privacy policy then applies.

## Children

BillBook is a general finance tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open BillBook, so protect your phone with a screen lock and keep your own backups using Export CSV.

## Your choices

- Delete individual bills or payments inside the app at any time.
- Delete all BillBook data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in your phone's settings.
- Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

## Changes to this policy

If BillBook starts collecting or sharing any data, we will update this page and the date above before releasing that version.
