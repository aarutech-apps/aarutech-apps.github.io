# TimeGuard Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

TimeGuard is a reminder app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** TimeGuard has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no server. We never receive, see or store any of your information.

## What stays on your device

The reminders you create (titles, notes, dates, categories and alert settings) are saved only in a local database on your phone. They never leave the device unless your phone's own backup (such as iCloud or Google backup) includes app data, which is controlled by you and your device settings.

Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** to show your reminders.
- **Alarms & reminders (Android):** so alerts arrive at the exact time.
- **Run at startup (Android):** to restore scheduled alerts after your phone restarts.

These permissions are used only to deliver your reminders.

## Children

TimeGuard does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
