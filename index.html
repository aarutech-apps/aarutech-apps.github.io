Our apps

One problem. One app. Done well.

Each AaruTech app does a single job and gets out of your way.

TimeGuard

Reminds you of everything - from bills to pills.

Never miss what matters. Set it once and TimeGuard keeps watch over your due dates, doses, renewals and routines.

  • Bill, subscription & renewal reminders
  • Medication schedules with gentle nudges
  • Recurring tasks and important dates
Google Play App Store - soon

Wainak

Helping lost pets find their way home.

When a pet goes missing, every minute counts. Wainak connects owners with neighbours who can help spot and return them fast.

  • Post a lost or found pet in seconds
  • Alerts to people nearby
  • Photos, last-seen location & safe contact
Google Play App Store - soon

BillBook

Bills and dues, for home and business.

Snap a bill and BillBook reads it for you. Track what's paid, what's still owed, and to whom - in a personal book or a business book.

  • Scan a bill - vendor, date, total and items filled in
  • Personal: what you owe · Business: purchases & sales
  • Part-payments, overdue flags and party balances
  • Fully offline - no account, data stays on your phone
Google Play App Store - soon
