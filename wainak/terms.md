## Purpose

Wainak lets pet owners alert nearby people when a pet is lost, and lets rescuers share sightings and
information. Everyone who registers can both report a lost pet and help others as a rescuer.

## Zero tolerance for misuse

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. You must not:

- Raise a false or prank alert, report someone else's pet as your own lost pet, or mark an animal as
  rescued when you have not rescued it. (Animals seen without an owner can be posted as "needs rescue".)
- Post harassing, hateful, sexual, violent or otherwise objectionable content, or anyone's private data.
- Use information from the app (including phone numbers shared with you) for anything other than reuniting a
  pet with its owner.
- Demand money or a reward in exchange for returning a pet, or sell or keep a pet you found.

Anyone can report an alert or a message and block a user from the app. We may share information with authorities
where required by law.

## Your content

You are responsible for what you post. You allow us to show it, with your name, to other users of the app for
the purpose of finding the pet. You can delete your content by deleting your account.

between users or with animals.
