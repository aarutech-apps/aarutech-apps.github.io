# Delete your Wainak account

[العربية](/ar/delete-account)

## In the app (instant)

1. Open Wainak and go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap **Delete my account** and confirm.

## By email

If you can no longer access the app, email [wainak.help@gmail.com](mailto:wainak.help@gmail.com) from any
address with the subject "Delete my account" and the email address and mobile number you registered with. We will verify
ownership and delete the account.

## What is deleted

- Your email address, mobile number, password, name, location and device push token.
- All messages and photos you posted in search chats.
- All lost-pet reports you created, including their photos and chats.
- Phone-number requests you sent or received.

Nothing is kept after deletion, except copies in the server's securely stored backups, which are deleted
automatically within 14 days. Backups are used only to restore the service after a failure.
