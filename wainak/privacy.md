
# Wainak Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 28 September 2026*

Wainak helps people find lost pets quickly. When an owner reports a lost pet, registered users nearby
("rescuers") are alerted and can share sightings in a search chat. This policy explains what we collect and why.

## Information we collect

- **Email address, mobile number and password**: to create your account and log in. Your email and number
  are never shown to other users; your number is shared only if you explicitly approve a request from a specific
  person. Passwords are stored only in hashed form. In a later version we may verify mobile numbers with a
  one-time SMS code.
- **Name**: shown next to your messages and your lost-pet reports, so owners and rescuers know who they
  are talking to.
- **Location**: your approximate current location while you use the app, so we can alert you only
  about pets lost near you. Locations you attach to a message are visible to people in that search.
- **Lost-pet and stray-animal reports**: the animal's photo, name (if known), type, breed, age,
  description and location, shown to users of the app. If you mark a stray as rescued, your name is shown as
  its rescuer.
- **Messages and photos** you post in search chats, visible to people helping in that search.
- **Device push token**: to deliver alerts to your phone.
- **App language**: to send you notifications in your language.

## How we use it

- To alert rescuers near a lost pet and to let people share information in the search chat.
- To notify owners and rescuers about new messages and phone-number requests.
- To prevent abuse (for example, limiting failed logins and how many alerts can be sent).

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

## Sharing

- Lost-pet reports and search chats (including your name) are visible to registered users of the app.
- Your phone number is shared only with a user whose request you approve.
- Service providers that process data on our behalf: our hosting provider and Expo / Google Firebase / Apple Push Notification service (to deliver notifications).
- Authorities, where required by applicable law.

## Your rights

You can delete everything yourself anytime in the app (see below) or contact us.

## Retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app: **Profile → Delete my account**. This permanently
removes your profile, your messages and photos, and any lost-pet reports you created. Copies may remain in the
server's backups for up to 14 days, after which they are deleted automatically. See also
[how to delete your account](/wainak/delete-account).

## Age

The app is intended for adults (18+).

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, and repeated failed
logins are blocked for a while. Photo metadata (such as the GPS position stored by cameras) is removed on upload.
